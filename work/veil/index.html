Santander

View live

Packaging and site design for a minimalist skincare line—clinical calm meets soft warmth, with a freelancer-led system from jar to checkout.

ROLE
Freelance Brand & Web Designer
TIMELINE
4 weeks
FOCUS
Beauty, D2C, Packaging

The Challenge

**Veil** had formulas they loved and lab partners lined up, but the brand felt like “another beige skincare drop.” They needed a story about clarity and gentle coverage—without clinical coldness or faux wellness poetry.

What I Did

I defined **Veil** as light structure for skin: simple steps, honest ingredients, no fake science graphs. I designed a packaging system with generous whitespace, one serif for warmth, one sans for clarity, and color coding per step so the shelf reads calm and intentional. The Shopify build mirrors that—routine builder, ingredient glossary, and PDPs that don’t bury the “why this bottle.”

Deliverables

Brand Architecture (Sub-Brand + Routine Steps)
Packaging System (Jar, Box, Travel)
Ingredient Story + Compliance Copy Tone
Shopify Product + Routine Pages
Photography Direction (Texture, Hand, Light)
Mini Guidelines PDF For Future SKUs
